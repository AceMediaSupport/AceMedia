## Template Quality Standards
- Professional branded headers with AceMedia colors
- Alternating row colors for readability
- Frozen header rows
- Pre-built formulas (SUM, averages, percentages)
- Data validation dropdowns where applicable
- Conditional formatting (color-coded status)
- Instructions tab on every template
- Print-ready formatting
- Works in Google Sheets AND Excel
